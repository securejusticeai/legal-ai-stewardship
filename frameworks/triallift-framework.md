# The TrialLift Framework
## Canonical Framework | AI Stewardship | Track 1: Legal & Organizational

---

## Definition

The TrialLift Framework is the architecture of Richard Schreiber's proprietary trial preparation platform: four integrated layers that validate AI-generated citations, quarantine hallucinations before they reach a filing, and build predictive trial strategy from case history, judge history, and opposing counsel patterns.

TrialLift is AI Stewardship expressed as software. The platform's founding premise is the canonical claim in operational form: AI as a human-controlled tool, with the governance layer built into the architecture rather than bolted on after the sanction.

---

## The Four Layers

### Layer 1 — Citation Analyzer
Every AI-generated citation validated against authoritative sources before it enters work product. Hallucinated authorities are not flagged for later review — they are quarantined, separated from the workflow so an unverified citation cannot travel from research to filing by momentum. (Operates the discipline defined in The Six-Step Citation Verification Protocol.)

### Layer 2 — Predictive Analytical Path
Trial strategy built from data the firm already has access to but has never been able to synthesize: case history, judge history, and opposing counsel patterns. The output is not a prediction engine replacing attorney judgment — it is the analytical foundation attorney judgment operates on.

### Layer 3 — Integration Layer
Connection into the firm's existing practice-management environment, because a trial preparation platform that requires a firm to abandon its current systems is a platform that never gets adopted. Built for the 10-15 attorney firm without a technology committee.

### Layer 4 — Governance Layer
The stewardship infrastructure: human-in-the-loop checkpoints, verification logging, and accountability records native to the platform. The layer that makes TrialLift defensible in front of the judge, the carrier, and the client.

---

## What It Is Not

- It is not a legal research tool with AI features. It is trial preparation architecture with governance as a structural layer.
- It is not a replacement for attorney judgment. Every layer terminates in a human decision.
- It is not aspirational. TrialLift is in active rollout (2026) with early client engagement documented in /proof.
